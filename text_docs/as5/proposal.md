**The shared problem.** A safety computation is a **chain of stages**, and an attack succeeds by breaking one link. Each sibling established a different link, black-box:

| Link | What breaks it | Established by |
|---|---|---|
| **Transmit** — does the payload arrive legibly? | image augmentation destroys legibility | AS-2 (ImgAug); OCR fidelity is the dose |
| **Decode** — does the stage invert the encoding? | pipeline recovers but never decodes | AS-3 (The Decode Gap) — guard-dependent: LlamaGuard-3 79→60, GuardReasoner-VL 71→89, three guards null |
| **Represent** — is harm represented once decoded? | — | *unmeasured black-box* |
| **Convert** — does representation become a block? | payload variation | AS-4 (Variance Channels): `end-to-end = boundary-pass × conversion`; gate 13→57 |

AS-5's four regimes are that same chain measured **inside the target**: can't decode (no decode link) / didn't decode (link exists, untraversed) / decoded-but-complied (represent ✓, convert ✗) / decoded-and-refused (chain intact). **AS-6 runs the same diagnostic on the defense instead of the target.** The motivating gap: all three siblings measure defense failure as end-to-end ASR, which structurally confounds *never received it* / *received but didn't decode* / *decoded but didn't flag*.

**Scope v1 — deliberately narrow.** Text guards (WildGuard, LlamaGuard-3), **two links: decode and convert**. One corpus, one guard set, one judge, with internals and behaviour measured on the same cells.

**The siblings supply hypotheses, NOT measurements.** Their pipelines differ on the target side (AS-2/AS-3 are VLM-target, AS-4 text-target; different payloads, defenses and judges), so stitching their numbers into one chain would be a confound a reviewer would rightly kill. AS-6 **re-measures the behavioural effect in its own harness** and then explains it internally — an extra behavioural arm, and a real cost. What makes the unification work is that the pipelines diverge *upstream* of AS-6's object: **LlamaGuard-3 is evaluated in BOTH AS-3 and AS-4**, and AS-3's decode gap — despite a VLM target — is a claim about a *text* guard receiving recovered-but-undecoded text.

**Declared extension, NOT in v1:** the vision channel and with it AS-2's **transmit** link (adding GuardReasoner-VL, with OCR fidelity as the dose-response control). Excluded because transmission is the *dominant* variable in the vision channel, so forcing all three links into one chain would repeat the framing error this cut exists to fix.

> **Correction 2026-08-05 (from the scoop check).** The original reason given here was "transmission is only a variable in the vision channel — in text the tokens simply arrive." That is **false as stated**, and the paper must not rest on it. **Prompt Overflow** (Zhou et al., arXiv 2605.23196, May 2026) is a transmit-link failure *in text*: guards inspect a bounded window (~512 tokens) while the downstream LLM integrates the full context, so fragmenting a payload across windows yields ~100% bypass with intent still reconstructible downstream. The exclusion still holds, but on a narrower and honest ground — v1 fixes prompt length below the guard's inspection window, which holds transmit constant *by construction* rather than by assumption, and that constraint must be stated as a scope condition (and checked per guard, since context limits differ). **The delta, in one sentence.** *Unlike SIREN (Jiao et al., arXiv 2604.18519), which probes guard-model internals to build a better detector on standard unencoded benchmarks, and DecipherGuard (Yang et al., arXiv 2509.16870), which shows behaviourally that guards fail on obfuscated prompts and that pre-decoding repairs them, AS-6 measures inside the guard WHICH LINK broke — separating "never decoded" from "decoded but never converted to a block" on identical payloads across an encoding ladder — turning an end-to-end DSR drop into a per-link attribution that says which repair a given guard actually needs.*

**What the check found is already taken** (each of these is now a must-cite, and none may be claimed as an AS-6 finding):
- **Probing a guard's own internals — the mechanism — exists.** SIREN's Appendix C probes LlamaGuard-3-8B and Qwen3Guard-4B themselves, lifting LlamaGuard-3-8B from 77.0 to 87.1 F1. Read carefully this is a **gift, not a threat**: it is independent evidence for AS-6's motivating premise — a guard's activations carry ~10 F1 points its output does not emit — measured by someone else, on unencoded data. AS-6's contribution therefore cannot be *"the gap exists"*; it must be *"the gap decomposes, and the decomposition is actionable"*.
- **"Guards fail on encoded prompts, and decoding repairs them" is established behaviourally.** DecipherGuard measures a 24–37% DSR drop under obfuscation- and template-based attacks and recovers 18.6pp with a decipher layer. It is the black-box analogue of the sibling's AS-3 Decode Gap and is a *prior* to it — flag to the AS-3 authors. AS-6 re-measures behaviour only to bind it to internals in one harness, never as a finding.
- **Represent-vs-act as a frame is established target-side** (*Knowing without Acting*, arXiv 2603.05773; *LLMs Encode Harmfulness and Refusal Separately*, arXiv 2507.11878). The conceptual move is not novel; the **object** (the guard) and the **decode link** are.
- **Gamma-Guard** (Lv et al., EMNLP 2025, pp. 12219–12231) — **DEEP-DIVED 2026-08-05, and DOWNGRADED from co-nearest prior.** PDF + text in science `literature/model-internals/papers/gamma-guard/`. It threatens AS-6 much less than its abstract suggests, on three verified counts: (i) its internal analysis is **qualitative and illustrative** — PCA scatter at layers 0 and 32, layer-wise safe/unsafe probability curves, one attention heat-map, on single illustrative samples, with **no quantitative shift metric, no aggregate statistic, no test**; (ii) all **12 attacks are standard adversarial-NLP** (ILGR, GCG, TextFooler, PWWS, GT, SPSO, BERT-Attack, BAE, FD, SEA, SCPN, GAN — character-perturbation, gradient word-substitution, sentence paraphrase) with **zero encoding or obfuscation attacks** — no base64, cipher, morse, zero-width, homoglyph, or translation rung anywhere; (iii) its causal story is *embedding noise → attention dilution → boundary drift*, which **never distinguishes "the guard did not decode it" from "the guard decoded it and did not flag it"** and makes no internal-representation-versus-output-decision claim at all.

  **Two gifts it hands AS-6, both citable:**
  1. **Where the guard commits its verdict, measured.** On Llama-Guard-3-8B the safe/unsafe probability curves for clean and attacked inputs are *indistinguishable through layer 27 of 32*, then diverge sharply in the final five layers. That sites AS-6's **convert**-link readout empirically rather than by guess, and it makes a sharp prediction for the **decode** link: any decoded-harm signal must be findable *before* layer 27, or the "decoded but not converted" cell cannot exist as described.
  2. **A published emptiness claim.** "No prior study tackles LLM Guard robustness, making Gamma-Guard the first" (EMNLP 2025) — useful supporting evidence that the guard-internals space was thin as of late 2025, to be cited as *their* claim, never asserted as ours.

  **And it sharpens our own delta:** doing this **quantitatively** — licensed probes, aggregate per-link attribution, controls — is itself part of AS-6's contribution, because the nearest work that looked inside a guard did it with three pictures.

## Line scope

Safety research inside model parameters — two halves, one pipeline:

- **Training-time:** safety fine-tuning, unlearning, tamper-resistance-style interventions.
- **Non-training internals:** activation steering, representation engineering, weight editing, internals-level analysis of safety behavior.

Model-size-agnostic: the pipeline is identical across scales; small open models are the default experimental substrate (cheaper runs, more seeds, same conclusions).
